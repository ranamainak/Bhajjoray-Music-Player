Privacy Policy – Bhajjoray

Last Updated: November 2026

Bhajjoray (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides a devotional bhajan player application and website available for users worldwide. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting user information.

Information We Collect

Bhajjoray does not require registration or personal information to access or use the App or Website. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or any sensitive personal data.

Location Information (Optional)

With your permission, the App accesses your approximate or precise device location solely to calculate accurate local Vedic Panchang timings and to detect your city in the Puja Store & Bhajan Organiser Finder. Location data is used only for these features in real time and is not stored on our servers, sold, or used for advertising or tracking. You may deny location permission and enter a city manually.

Kundali & Birth Chart Inputs

Date, time, and place of birth entered in the Birth Chart (Kundali) feature are processed locally on your device to generate charts and are not transmitted to or stored on Bhajjoray servers.

Notifications & Alarms (Optional)

If enabled, notification and alarm permissions are used solely for local Vrat & Festival reminders and scheduled Autoplay.

Usage & Diagnostics

Basic non-personal technical data such as crash logs or performance analytics may be automatically provided by your device or Google Play to help maintain and improve service stability. This information cannot be used to identify you.

Limited Advertising

These ads may be served through Google Ads / Google AdMob, which may use non-personal technical identifiers to manage ad performance and avoid repetition. Bhajjoray does not collect or store any personally identifiable information for advertising purposes. Users may control ad personalization from the device or Google account settings.
