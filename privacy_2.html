Privacy Policy for Bhajjoray Website Android App

Last Updated: October 22, 2025

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Bhajjoray handles information for both our public website (“Website”) and our Android application (“App”) collectively referred to as the “Services.”

Information We Collect

No personal information required: You can browse and listen to bhajans on our Services without providing your name, email address, phone number, or similar identifiers.

  • Website: We do not directly collect personal data. See “Advertising & Cookies” below regarding third-party cookies used for ads.
  • Android App: We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal details. The App does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Advertising & Cookies

  • Website (Google AdSense): We display ads using Google AdSense, which may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Android App (Google AdMob): Our App may show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device’s Advertising ID and similar technologies for ad delivery and frequency capping. You can reset or limit ad personalization from your Android device settings.

Log Data & Diagnostics

To maintain and improve reliability, standard, non-personal diagnostic information may be provided by your device or platform (for example, basic crash reports or ANR data supplied by Google Play). We do not use these signals to identify you.

Third-Party Links

Our Services may contain links to third-party platforms such as Facebook or YouTube. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We recommend reviewing those policies when you leave our Services.

Children’s Privacy

Our Services are suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. Please review this page periodically for the latest information.

Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, write to us at query@bhajjoray.com.
